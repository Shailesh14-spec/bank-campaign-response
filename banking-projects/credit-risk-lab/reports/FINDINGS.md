# Measured findings

Validation Brier score selected **gradient_boosting**. Untouched test ROC AUC is **0.780** and average precision is **0.554** against prevalence 0.221. Brier score is **0.1345**, compared with 0.1723 for the training-prior baseline.

The validation threshold flags 20.2% of test profiles and captures 52.3% of observed defaults. This is a research review policy, not a lending decision.

The baseline one-month loss proxy is TWD 29,438,726 at an assumed 45% LGD. Bill balance is only a proxy for exposure; this is not an IFRS 9, lifetime, regulatory-capital or validated economic forecast. Odds shifts are sensitivity assumptions.

Recommendation: retain the benchmark as evidence of modelling and calibration methods. Recent local, out-of-time data, validated exposure/recovery inputs, policy review and broader fairness testing would be needed before any banking use. The source is one historical Taiwanese cohort and cannot establish UK portfolio performance.
